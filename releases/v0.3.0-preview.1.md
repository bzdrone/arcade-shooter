# Arcade Shooter v0.3.0-preview.1

Build in MakeCode Arcade, then try your supported map and rules in Battlefield 6 Portal using the new browser converter.

## Start here

You need only two downloads:

1. **INTRO.md** — open this first for beginner tutorials, project-PNG import instructions, controls, and a first experiment.
2. **arcade-shooter.png** — the editable game. At https://arcade.makecode.com/, choose **Import → Import File** and open the original downloaded PNG. It opens in Blocks.

You do not need Git, a local toolchain, or GitHub's automatic **Source code** archives. The PNG contains the project, not just a picture. Do not re-encode it through an image editor or replace it with a screenshot.

Use the arrow keys to move and hold **Space / A** to shoot. Start with the introduction's one-number damage experiment; the larger map and Portal conversion are optional next steps.

## What's new since v0.2.0-preview.2

- **A larger sample map:** `combatCompound` adds rooms, doorways, courtyard cover, and flanking routes. Its 25×19 tiles scroll with the player in Arcade. Both sample maps are packaged in the PNG; the compact `trainingYard` remains the default. Select the compound in **set tilemap to** to try it, without changing the mode.
- **Experience modifier blocks:** set everyone's maximum health, incoming damage percent, respawn seconds, and friendly fire before startup. The baseline is 100 health, 100% incoming damage, five-second respawns, and friendly fire off. The introduction and first activity now use these settings rather than weapon timing.
- **PNG → Portal files:** save your edited shooter project as a PNG, then drop it on [Arcade to Portal](https://bzdrone.github.io/arcade-shooter/). Conversion runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. It produces map edits (`.spatial.json`), a TypeScript rules script (`.ts`), and messages (`.strings.json`).
- **Maps and modes transfer together:** supported tilemaps become walls, cover, and team spawns on Portal Sandbox. Deathmatch, Gun Game, and supported creator Mod rules are translated with a Portal runtime. The compound has a 100×76 m footprint in Portal.
- **Readable Portal scores:** converted scripts use a high-contrast top-centre score panel rather than the clipped notification header.

The converter is a separately hosted, experimental tool; it is not embedded in the PNG. It supports this shooter's maps and rules, not arbitrary Arcade games. Unsupported features are reported rather than guaranteed to transfer. Arcade remains a top-down starting environment; Portal uses first-person movement and real weapons.

## Checks and limits

- The candidate is the original validated baseline PNG. Its embedded implementation, settings, block structure, and map assets were checked against the committed game sources, allowing only the documented editor-generated presentation differences.
- Arcade compilation, host gameplay/pathing checks, converter/runtime tests, site packaging, and generated-script checks against Portal SDK 2.1.2.0 passed.
- Existing fresh local and hosted Arcade import evidence matches these exact PNG bytes: Blocks-first opening, custom blocks/help, simulator startup, and editable tilemaps. Local import checks also exercised a damage-setting change; hosted checks exercised Gun Game. This preparation reuses that import evidence rather than claiming a new full gameplay or beginner trial.
- The live converter was checked at desktop and phone sizes with a compound Gun Game PNG, including previews and all three output files.
- In a focused author playtest in Battlefield 6 Portal, compound Gun Game weapon progression worked and the score was readable. Weapon-level persistence across respawn and the final knife-elimination win were **not confirmed in live play**.

This is a **preview**, not full Portal compatibility or validated teaching material. Broader Portal Builder workflows, arbitrary custom rules, full natural matches, and creator usability remain unverified. SDK-backed modifier behavior is not a mirror of Portal Builder multipliers.

After editing settings in Arcade, **stop the simulator and press Play** to recompile; fast restart may reuse old code. Instructions are separate from the PNG. Optional activities and the [MIT license](https://github.com/bzdrone/arcade-shooter/blob/main/LICENSE) remain online in the repository.
